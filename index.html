How do you connect it?

Plug a normal 3.5 mm AUX cable (the kind with a small headphone-style plug on each end) from the projector's sound output into the soundbar. Put the soundbar below the screen. The cable is usually easier to keep in sync with voices than Bluetooth.

Step 3 · Where the picture goes

Do we need a screen or backboard?

Not necessarily! You can project straight onto a smooth, light-coloured wall. If you want a neater finish, choose one of these screens instead.

Smooth white wall

Lowest cost

If the wall is flat and smooth, this can look very good for the money. Fill any dents and use matte white paint. Paint and preparation cost extra.

The easy size guide

How big a picture will fit?

The closer the projector is to the screen, the smaller the picture. Both projectors need almost the same placement distance. The distance is measured from the front lens to the wall or screen.

You could get roughly
90-inch

A picture about 2.0 metres wide. A sofa roughly 2.7–3.7 metres away is a useful starting point.

This is a nice size for either projector in a dark room.

On setup day

Four easy steps to movie night.

Nothing complicated. A few small decisions make a bigger difference than fancy extras.

📏

1. Check the distance

Measure where the projector can go. Use the table above to choose the right screen size before buying.

🌙

2. Darken the room

Turn off lights and close curtains. This helps both projectors look much better.

🔊

3. Put sound at the front

Place the soundbar under the screen. Connect it to the projector with an AUX cable.

🍿

4. Test before mounting

Try the projector on a safe table first. Check the image fits and keep the air vents clear.
