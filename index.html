Connecting the sound
Use a 3.5 mm AUX cable (a headphone-style cable) between the projector's audio output and the soundbar's AUX input. A cable usually avoids Bluetooth sound delay. The projector's built-in speakers are also an option.

Setup checklist

1. Measure

Check the lens-to-screen distance and the clear wall width.

2. Control light

Use curtains or blinds to keep the room dark.

3. Connect audio

Place the soundbar below the screen and use an AUX cable.

4. Test first

Check image size and focus before mounting. Leave vents clear.
